Technical Details

Preserved Features:
  • ✅ Squared shape with 12px border-radius
  • ✅ Glassmorphism with backdrop-filter blur (4px → 6px on hover)
  • ✅ Transparency and bubble look with inset shadows
  • ✅ Scale (1.15×) and lift (-4px) on hover
  • ✅ Smooth cubic-bezier transitions (0.4s)
Color Changes:
  • 🎨 Border: Grey shadow (normal) → Blue (hover)
  • 🎨 Center: Very light baby blue (normal) → Brighter blue (hover)
  • 🎨 Glow: Grey shadows (normal) → Blue glow (hover)
  • 🎨 Radial gradient: Grey edge fading to blue center
