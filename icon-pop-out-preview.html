Icon Pop-Out Preview

Interactive dropdown menus with glassmorphism design. Click the icons in the header to see the dropdowns in action!

🔍 Search Dropdown

Click the search icon to open a centered, transparent search bar with iOS-style glassmorphism. The background blurs and an overlay appears.

Features: Centered position, large input field, background blur, click outside to close

🛒 Cart Dropdown

Click the cart icon to see your shopping cart with items and prices. The dropdown opens on the right side with a subtle white tint for text visibility.

Features: 300×400px size, shows items and prices, total calculation, checkout button, 1% white tint (rgba 0.96)

👤 Account Dropdown

Click the account icon to access the login form. Enter your email and password, or click "Registrieren" to create a new account.

Features: Login form, German text (Anmelden, Registrieren), same glass design as cart

✨ Design Features

Fixed Header: Icons stay at the top when scrolling

Glassmorphism: Semi-transparent backgrounds with backdrop-filter blur

iOS-Style: Glass lens effect on icons with curved highlights

Smooth Animations: Slide-down and fade-in transitions

Click Outside: Closes active dropdown intuitively

Instructions

1. Click any icon in the header to open its dropdown

2. Click outside the dropdown or press ESC to close it

3. Only one dropdown can be open at a time

4. Scroll the page to see the fixed header behavior

5. Try hovering over the icons to see the glass lens effect

Implementation Notes

This preview demonstrates the interactive dropdown system that can be integrated into production. All dropdowns use glassmorphism with background blur, creating an iOS-style appearance. The design is production-ready and can be used on all subpages.
